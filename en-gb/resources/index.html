Frequently asked questions

Answers with the evidence boundary included.

Is OLANG a manufacturer or a trading company?

OLANG’s public company profile describes a Foshan-based bathroom hardware manufacturer. This website reproduces that self-description and marks it as company-reported. Company registration, factory audit documents and current production evidence are available only after OLANG confirms them.

What is the MOQ?

A verified MOQ is not published for these SKUs. MOQ is confirmed per product, finish and order mix after the buying list is reviewed. The inquiry form lets you provide target quantities so the team can respond with a specific answer rather than a generic figure.

Can I request a sample?

Sample availability, sample cost and courier arrangements are not published. Submit the product, finish, quantity and destination details; the team will confirm whether a sample can be supplied and on what terms.

Can you customize finish, logo or packaging?

OLANG publicly describes OEM/ODM and private-label support. This website does not claim that every SKU or process is available for customization. Submit the target product, changes, quantities and market; feasibility, tooling, MOQ and timing are confirmed in writing.

Are the products certified for my market?

No blanket certification is claimed. CE, UKCA, WaterMark, ADA, WRAS, REACH or other requirements apply to specific products and markets. Any statement must be supported by a valid document covering the exact SKU. Use the document request to ask for the compliance scope.

Why are prices not shown?

These products are quoted against specification, quantity, finish, packing and delivery terms. No verified public price list is available, so showing a template price would be misleading. Submit a buying list and the team will quote the exact configuration.

How do I get a specification sheet?

Specification sheets are released per SKU and can require review by the company. Add the product model and requested document in the document request form. Public downloads are shown only when a real file has been uploaded and approved; no placeholder PDF links are used.

Can I upload a drawing or confidential file?

Yes, but share only what is necessary. Uploads are stored outside the public web root and are visible only to authorised staff. If the drawing is confidential, request an NDA before uploading. Files are type/size checked, but this deployment does not claim antivirus scanning.

What is the lead time?

No verified production or delivery lead time is published. It depends on the SKU, finish, quantity, packing and shipping route. The team confirms timing after reviewing the specification and destination.
