Privacy & cookies

How we handle your information.

This notice explains the data collected when you browse this website, submit a procurement inquiry or use the product assistant.

Policy version 2026-11-08 · Updated 2026-11-08

1. Data controller

OLANG, through Foshan Langhao Hardware, is responsible for the personal data submitted through this website. Privacy questions can be sent to master@olang.cn or through the privacy request form.

2. Data we collect

Inquiry forms: name, company, business email, phone if provided, country and region, product models and quantities, project and delivery requirements, and any files you attach. The server also records the source page, submission time and a one-way hash of the request IP for security and abuse prevention.

Optional analytics: page path, event type, market edition, product model, anonymous visitor and session identifiers, device class, referring page and country code when supplied by the hosting service. Raw IP addresses are not stored in analytics.

Product assistant: the question is stored in redacted form with the market edition, matched record and time. Do not enter confidential personal data into the assistant.

3. Why we use it

Inquiry data is used to review and respond to your business request, confirm specifications and documents, prepare a quotation and prevent spam. Optional analytics helps us understand which market editions and product pages are useful.

We do not sell personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

4. Cookies and storage

Essential cookies support security, consent, form protection and authenticated staff sessions. Analytics storage is optional and is not activated before consent. Your procurement list is stored in your browser until you submit it.

5. Retention

Inquiry records are retained for up to 24 months after the last contact unless a legal obligation or contract requires longer. Attachments follow the same schedule or the shorter upload retention of 12 months. Anonymous analytics are retained for up to 400 days. Staff sessions expire after 8 hours.

6. Recipients and transfers

Authorised OLANG staff and, where configured, the email service provider used to notify them can access inquiry data. A hosting, database or email provider may process data in another country; appropriate safeguards are required before launch.

Uploaded drawings and commercial files are stored in a private directory and are served only to authenticated staff.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, and may withdraw analytics consent at any time through Cookie settings. Use the privacy request route or contact master@olang.cn. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

Forms use server-side validation, CSRF protection, rate limiting and role-based access to the staff portal. Passwords are stored with a salted scrypt hash. Uploads are type- and size-checked and stored outside the public web root. Files are not published.
