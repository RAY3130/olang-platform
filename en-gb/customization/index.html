Development process

The six-stage customization process

The original OLANG process is preserved. Each stage shows what the buyer provides, what OLANG reviews and what must be confirmed before the next stage.

01

Consultation and needs analysis

Buyer provides
Product target, intended use, market, quantity and any reference material.
OLANG reviews
Clarifies the request and checks the published product record.
Confirm before next stage
The brief is complete enough for a design review.
02

Custom design and development

Buyer provides
Drawings, reference images, dimensions or a sample.
OLANG reviews
Reviews material, structure, finish, tooling and feasibility.
Confirm before next stage
The design direction and technical questions.
03

Confirm and finalize design plan

Buyer provides
Feedback on the proposal.
OLANG reviews
Updates the drawing, scope and commercial assumptions.
Confirm before next stage
Final drawing and written scope before sampling.
04

Sample production

Buyer provides
Approves the sample request and receiving details.
OLANG reviews
Produces the sample against the agreed specification.
Confirm before next stage
Sample fee, timing and delivery route.
05

Sample inspection

Buyer provides
Checks appearance, fit, packing and function.
OLANG reviews
Records sample findings and agreed changes.
Confirm before next stage
Written sample approval before mass production.
06

Mass production and delivery

Buyer provides
Confirms order, packing and shipping instructions.
OLANG reviews
Produces, inspects, packs and prepares shipping documents.
Confirm before next stage
Inspection release and shipping documents.

Sample timing, tooling, MOQ and production schedules are confirmed in the project proposal.
