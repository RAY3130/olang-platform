Who it is for

Built for repeatable range buying.

This route is designed for businesses that buy multiple SKUs, need consistent packing and expect a documented quotation. It does not publish a generic MOQ or lead time because no verified values are available.

  • Importers building a mixed container or first trial order
  • Wholesalers and regional distributors planning a repeatable range
  • Bathroom showrooms and retail buyers coordinating finishes
  • Project suppliers needing a documented product list

What to include

The details that change the answer.

  • SKU or model, quantity and required finish per item
  • Target market, shipping destination and preferred Incoterm
  • Inner box, master carton, mixed-SKU packing or private label
  • Documents required for your customer or customs process
  • Expected order date and any phased delivery requirement
