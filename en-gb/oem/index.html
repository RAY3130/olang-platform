Scope to discuss

Customization is a project decision, not a catalogue switch.

OLANG supports OEM, ODM and private-label projects. Each request is reviewed against the target product, tooling, materials, finish, quantities and market before sampling.

01

Existing product adaptation

Change of finish, dimensions, mounting detail, packaging or branding on an existing published model, subject to review.

02

Material & finish requirements

Target material, grade, coating and test requirements are reviewed against available capability and evidence.

03

Drawing-led development

Share a drawing or sample reference. Confidential files can be held until an NDA is agreed.

04

Branding & packaging

Logo, inner box, carton marks, manuals and barcode data are specified in the approved project file.

Do not send confidential drawings before the NDA route is agreed.

Uploads are stored outside the public web root and are available only to authorised staff. For confidential drawings, select the NDA option and wait for written confirmation before uploading.

Development process

Seven stages, with a documented decision at each gate.

01

Brief

Target product, market and change.

02

Review

Drawing, sample and technical feasibility.

03

Proposal

Tooling, MOQ, sample and timing proposal.

04

NDA / IP

Confidentiality and design ownership confirmed.

05

Sample

Sample made and checked against the brief.

06

Approval

Specification, packaging and QC criteria signed off.

07

Production

Batch production and delivery planning.

Sample timing, tooling, MOQ and production schedules are confirmed in the project proposal.
